# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Icthus exists to meet people where they are. That has to be true of the project
as well as the product.

Everyone is welcome here regardless of background, tradition, denomination,
level of faith or doubt, nationality, disability, age, gender, or experience.
You do not need to hold any particular belief to contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Welcome before labels. This project does not argue denominations, and
  contributors are not asked about theirs.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that reasonable people disagree, including about theology, and that a
  disagreement does not need a winner.
- Remember what this codebase holds. Discussions about privacy, safety, and
  crisis handling deserve care rather than speed.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Proselytising at other contributors, or treating someone's tradition,
  doubt, or lack of belief as a defect.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sharing real user content in an issue, a pull request, or a screenshot. Use
  synthetic fixtures — this is a hard rule, not a preference.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **conduct@bahrcompany.com**. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers will respond as promptly as they can and will not retaliate against
anyone who reports in good faith.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour
they consider harmful.
